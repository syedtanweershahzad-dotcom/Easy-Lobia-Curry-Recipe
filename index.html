Easy Lobia Curry Recipe

Flavor packed and wholesome, this Lobia curry aka black eyed peas curry is a great meal to make for the entire family.

No ratings yet
Course Main Course
Cuisine Indian
Prep 10 min
Cook 20 min
Total 30 min
Servings 5
Calories 153 kcal
Author Syed Tanweer Shahzad

Ingredients

12 items
  • 2 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • ½ tsp Cumin Seeds
  • ¼ tsp Fenugreek Seeds
  • 1 tbsp Dried Fenugreek Leaves
  • 1-2 Green Chilies (slit)
  • ½ cup Onions (sliced)
  • ½ cup Potatoes (diced)
  • ½ cup Carrots (chopped)
  • ½ cup Beans (chopped)
  • 1 cup Vegetable Stock
  • 1 pinch Turmeric Powder
  • ½ tsp Red Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 cup Black Eyed Beans (soaked & boiled)
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • Coriander Leaves (chopped)

Instructions

8 steps
  1. 1
    Soak & Cook Beans

    Soak black eyed beans overnight (or 3-4 hours). Pressure cook for 6-7 whistles until tender. Drain and set aside.

  2. 2
    Heat Oil & Add Spices

    Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds, fenugreek seeds, green chilies, and dried fenugreek leaves.

  3. 3
    Sauté Vegetables

    Add onions, potatoes, carrots, and beans. Sauté for 2-3 minutes until slightly tender.

  4. 4
    Add Stock & Simmer

    Pour in vegetable stock, bring to a boil, then simmer for 5-6 minutes.

  5. 5
    Add Powdered Spices

    Add turmeric, red chili powder, and garam masala. Mix well.

  6. 6
    Add Beans & Salt

    Add boiled black eyed beans and salt. Stir gently.

  7. 7
    Simmer & Adjust

    Simmer for 5-6 more minutes. Add water or stock to adjust consistency.

  8. 8
    Garnish & Serve

    Adjust seasoning, garnish with coriander leaves, and serve hot with rice or roti.
